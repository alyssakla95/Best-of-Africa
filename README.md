# BOA-Story

> A digital home for real, thoughtful stories about African lives, cities, and ideas — beyond charity ads and disaster headlines.

## Vision

BOA-Story is a **living digital platform** built to surface real, grounded stories about African lives, cities, creators, and everyday opportunity. It is explicitly positioned *against* the dominant media framing of Africa as a place of crisis, charity, and disaster.

It is not a news outlet, not a charity, and not a personal blog. It is a **narrative correction platform** — a media and editorial product being built in public, iteratively, by a student and independent writer.

The project is small and self-funded. The goal is to close the gap between the Africa seen in headlines and the Africa heard about from friends, founders, and family.

---

## The Platform

BOA-Story is currently in a **prototype and pre-launch stage**. The platform leverages a backend content engine to help surface stories, but the editorial voice remains strictly human-centric, grounded, and independent. 

### Core Sections
- **Stories**: Narrative journalism and deep-dives.
- **Supporter Feed**: Behind-the-scenes updates and early access drafts.
- **Gallery**: Visual curation of life and opportunity across the continent.
